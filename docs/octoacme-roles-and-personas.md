# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, create comprehensive test plans, oversee testing activities, and report quality metrics to the project team. They ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and maintain high quality throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria for features
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Conduct testing (manual and automated) across development phases
- Report quality metrics and test coverage to the team
- Identify defects and collaborate with developers on resolutions
- Advocate for quality and drive continuous improvement in testing processes

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet quality standards before release
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and traceability
- Reduce defects escaping to production
- Enable faster, more confident deployments

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on test coverage strategies, reviews technical approaches for testability, participates in code reviews from a quality perspective
- **Reports to Project Manager**: Provides quality metrics, flags quality risks, ensures quality gates are met before proceeding to next phases
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Validates that features meet acceptance criteria and user expectations
- **Supports Project Delivery**: Participates in sprint planning to estimate testing effort and identify quality risks early

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Quality metrics reports and dashboards
- Defect logs and quality risk assessments
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Code review feedback on testability

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system architecture, evaluate technical solutions, ensure scalability and performance, and provide technical guidance to the development team. They bridge product vision with technical feasibility and long-term system health.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical solutions for new features
- Review and approve technical approaches for alignment with architectural principles
- Ensure solutions meet scalability, performance, and reliability requirements
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and best practices
- Evaluate technology choices and dependencies

### Goals
- Maintain a scalable, performant, and maintainable architecture
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Enable rapid feature delivery without compromising system health
- Support long-term platform sustainability and growth

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Reviews code architecture, provides technical guidance, approves major design decisions
- **Advises Project Manager**: Assesses technical feasibility, estimates architectural impact on timelines, flags technical risks
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Ensures architectural decisions support product strategy and scalability needs
- **Partners with Quality Assurance**: Identifies architectural approaches that support testability and quality

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and diagrams
- Technical design reviews and approval sign-offs
- Code review comments on architectural alignment
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Participation in planning and design discussions

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers ensure smooth adoption of new features and process changes by managing stakeholder communication, planning change rollout strategies, and tracking adoption metrics. They minimize resistance and maximize user adoption of project deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management and communication plans
- Identify stakeholders affected by project changes
- Create and deliver stakeholder communication (emails, docs, training)
- Plan change rollout and phased release strategies
- Track adoption metrics and user feedback
- Address concerns and resistance to change
- Coordinate training and enablement activities

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption of new features and changes
- Minimize disruption and resistance during transitions
- Ensure clear communication across all stakeholder groups
- Track and measure adoption success

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Product Managers**: Aligns on messaging and positioning, incorporates product vision into communication
- **Coordinates with Project Manager**: Aligns change rollout timeline with project schedule, manages stakeholder escalations
- **Engages with Developers**: Communicates technical changes and technical rationale to non-technical audiences
- **Partners with Quality Assurance**: Incorporates quality and testing messaging into stakeholder communications

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and communication calendars
- Stakeholder communication materials and FAQs
- Training documentation and enablement resources
- Adoption metrics and feedback reports
- Participation in stakeholder briefings and launch planning

---

## Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance Officers ensure that projects adhere to regulatory requirements, organizational policies, and compliance standards. They audit project processes, maintain compliance documentation, and advise on regulatory impacts of project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review project requirements for regulatory and compliance implications
- Audit project processes and deliverables for compliance adherence
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit trails
- Advise on regulatory impacts and mitigation strategies
- Identify compliance risks and propose control measures
- Coordinate with legal and governance functions as needed

### Goals
- Ensure all project deliverables meet regulatory and compliance requirements
- Minimize compliance risks and potential violations
- Maintain audit readiness and documentation integrity
- Support organizational governance objectives

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reviews with Project Manager**: Assesses project deliverables against compliance requirements, escalates compliance risks, advises on compliance-related timeline implications
- **Advises on Technical Decisions**: Evaluates technical approaches for compliance implications (e.g., data security, privacy, retention)
- **Engages with Developers**: Reviews code and architecture for compliance concerns, advises on secure coding and data handling practices
- **Aligns with Product Managers**: Ensures features comply with regulatory requirements, advises on compliance-driven constraints

### Typical Communication
- Compliance assessments and risk reports
- Audit findings and remediation plans
- Regulatory impact analyses
- Compliance documentation and audit trails
- Participation in project reviews and compliance check-ins
